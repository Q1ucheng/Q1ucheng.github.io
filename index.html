Qiucheng Zhao 赵邱诚

I am currently a first-year master's student at the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), where my research focuses on the control of lower-limb hip exoskeleton robots. I am particularly interested in end-to-end hip joint torque estimation algorithms based on surface electromyography (sEMG) signals.

Previously, I received my B.E. in Mechatronic Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU), where I studied human–robot interaction in rehabilitation robotics.

Beyond my academic pursuits, I enjoy fitness and coffee. I am also an enthusiastic beginner in photography, currently using Lumix G97. If you are interested in photography or use the M43 system, I would be delighted to exchange ideas with you!

A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step ! 😉
